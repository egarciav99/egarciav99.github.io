CoreIT

Automation hub for companies · Own product

Problem: repetitive tasks, like turning technical PDF procedures into Excel capture sheets, eat hours and cause omissions.
What I built: a one-tap, POS-style hub where each team triggers its own automations. First case, for a quality-control lab: a pipeline that extracts the procedure twice in parallel (regex rules and an LLM), audits both against the original text with a second model, lets the user review and edit the result, and generates a protected Excel sheet with automatic completeness checks. I also prototyped a Python ETL that deduplicates the lab's reagent inventory into golden records with fuzzy matching.
Result: prototype presented to the client; next step, a multi-company SaaS with per-company branding.

Bocado AI

AI nutrition app · Collaboration (I led all the tech)

Problem: planning meals that fit your goals, allergies and pantry, and knowing where to eat out.
What I built: all the technology: a Next.js PWA that generates recipes from what you have at home and recommends real restaurants verified with Google Places, plus the ingredient database behind it (a Python/pandas ETL that consolidated 1,076 ingredients and their dietary restrictions into PostgreSQL).
Technical decisions: Gemini with forced JSON output, Firebase Auth and Firestore, per-user rate limiting, scheduled Cloud Functions with push notifications, Sentry and Vitest/Playwright tests. It evolved from an n8n + Airtable prototype.
Result: MVP born from a master's thesis with 200+ real users.

CoverCraft

AI cover letter generator · Personal SaaS

Problem: tailoring a cover letter to every job posting takes time.
What I built: upload your CV, paste the job posting and get an editable letter you can export to PDF.
Technical decisions: Next.js with Supabase (Auth, Storage and Row Level Security) and an asynchronous n8n pipeline: a parser that detects CV sections in 4 languages, Gemini with a fallback model and prompt rules that mirror the job's keywords for ATS filters, and a secret-protected callback.
Result: in real use; next step, a freemium launch.

PDF Technical Assistant

RAG assistant for technical documentation

Problem: technical documentation lives in long PDFs and it is hard to find the exact answer quickly.
What I built: upload a PDF, get a summary and chat with it; answers come only from what the document says.
Technical decisions: personal data is redacted before indexing, section-aware chunking, Gemini embeddings in pgvector with per-user isolation (RLS), and an agent that searches the document through function calling. Migrated from a multi-provider n8n workflow (Gemini, Azure OpenAI and a FastAPI microservice) to Supabase Edge Functions.
Result: working; in active development to adapt it to any company's documents.
